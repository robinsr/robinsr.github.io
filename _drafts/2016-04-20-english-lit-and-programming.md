Growing up I really wanted to be an architect, and I would create reams of drawings for the projects I would one day build. In college I shifted towards engineering, then seemingly out of nowhere English literature. I excelled at analyzing a subject and creating an argument to explain it, fix it, or just make sense of it. After college I shifted towards programming, and it is a natural fit. To me at least, programming works in ways very similar to both architecture and academic writing; the way you create the work and the way you experience the work are fundamentally different. You would layout a building with a 2D floorplan but you would experience in real space, without the high level view of what the whole is. Likewise you can think about acedemic writing in the same vein; You craft the experience with knowledge of the whole, but you experiene it one room at a time.

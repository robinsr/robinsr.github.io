About Me

My name is Ryan.

Thanks for being interested in me, you obviously have good taste in people. I code websites; kinda for a living, kinda for fun. My stack is LMKN or as I call it "lumpkin" (dont look it up on urbandictionary): Linux, MongoDB, Knockout, and Nodejs. When Im not contracting you might see me around west seattle motorbiking or coffee-ing. I am a graduate of WWU where I studied English lit and got really into post-modern critical theory (essentially I wrote a bunch of papers about the societal control structures operating on Wikipedia).

My blog is essentially my note taking space; I refer to my blog many times for things I cant keep in my head but I also strive to make the material accessible to beginner devs. It reflects not only my interests but also topics that I've struggled with and had to do some real research on. For this reason you might find it useful too!
